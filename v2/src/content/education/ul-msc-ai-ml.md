One-year full-time programme at the University of Limerick (NFQ Level 9), running across three semesters. Autumn core modules: Introduction to Data Engineering and Machine Learning (CE4051), Evolutionary Computation and Humanoid Robotics (CS6271), Text Analytics and Natural Language Processing (EE6041), Artificial Intelligence for Games (CS4096), and Machine Vision (CE6021). Spring core modules: Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (CE6012), Deep Reinforcement Learning (CS6482), Probabilistic and Explainable AI (CS6462), Theory and Practice of Advanced AI Ecosystems (CS5024), and Research Methods and Project Specification (ED5073). Summer capstone: the Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Project (CS6143) — a dissertation or learning project on a self-chosen research topic.

Directly building on the CS fundamentals from the BSc, going deeper into the machine learning, robotics, NLP, vision and AI-ecosystem areas that matter most for the software work I want to do long-term.
