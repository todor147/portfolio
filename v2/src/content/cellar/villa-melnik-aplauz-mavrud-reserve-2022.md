Mavrud's traditional vineyards are in Thrace, around Asenovgrad and Perushtitsa near Plovdiv, so a Reserve grown in the Struma Valley — Bulgaria's far southwest, not far from the Greek border — is already a slightly unexpected bottle. Villa Melnik makes it in its AplauZ range, the same line as the Melnik 55 elsewhere in this cellar.

## History

Villa Melnik was founded by Nikola and Lyubka Zikatanov, who planted their vineyards in 2004 and opened a gravity-flow winery near the village of Harsovo in 2013. Nikola was born in Kapatovo, the village just across from the winery, into a family with long winegrowing traditions, and the couple's stated mission is to bring back the reputation of Melnik wine. AplauZ is the estate's Reserve series of single-varietal wines, and this bottle wears the range's newer label — a stylised sun over mountain peaks — rather than the crest-and-village design still seen on the AplauZ Melnik 55.

## Region & terroir

The estate's vines grow on south-facing hillsides around Harsovo, about 7 km south of Melnik, in mostly sandy soils with some limestone and volcanic deposits, according to Wine Anorak. Officially this Mavrud is labelled under the broad PGI Thracian Lowlands rather than the local PGI Melnik used for the estate's Melnik 55. Villa Melnik is one of very few producers growing Mavrud in the Struma Valley — quite possibly the only one.

## The grape

Mavrud is one of Bulgaria's best-known indigenous reds: small-berried and late-ripening, capable of tannic, spicy wines with real ageing potential. Like Shiroka Melnishka, it needs a long season, and Villa Melnik says the Struma's warm summers and mild autumns let it reach full sugar and tannin ripeness here, giving wines it describes as dense, soft and fruit-forward.

## Winemaking

Villa Melnik ages it in Bulgarian oak barrels — 12 months for the 2021, and as long as 18 months for this 2022 by some accounts — comfortably beyond the AplauZ range's minimum of nine months in Bulgarian, French or American oak for its reds.

## Tasting notes

**Nose:** Black fruit and roasted pepper, with a savoury tomato-salsa edge and earthy undertones, going by the producer's notes for the current release.

**Palate:** Dense and powerful, with chocolate, sour cherry and a hint of tobacco, and enough balance to keep the weight in check.

**Finish:** Clean and intense, with the length the producer singles out as one of its strengths.

## Serving & pairing

Decant before serving, as the producer advises, and drink at 15–18°C. Villa Melnik suggests pork, wild boar and lamb — rich dishes that can stand up to its density.

## Why it's in the cellar

It is Mavrud from a valley where almost nobody else grows it, made by an estate better known for Melnik's own grapes. Set beside the AplauZ Melnik 55 from the same winery and the same 2022 vintage, it shows two very different reds from one valley: a Thracian classic grown far from home, and a grape bred locally.
